# How do I view service accounts of a project?

Open **Settings → Administration → Projects management**, open the project, and go to the
**Members** tab. In the **Account type** filter, select **Service Account**. Only service
accounts linked to the project are listed, each marked with a **SERVICE ACCOUNT** badge and its
budget and spend in the **Budget Allocations** column. An account without usage shows `$0.00`.

Only members of the project, Platform Admins, Maintainers, and Auditors can view its members.
Linking a service account to a project is performed by Platform Administrators or Support.
